Identity Verification (KYC)

Identity verification is required for every buyer and seller on PakGig. It protects both sides of every order.

Upload your photos (3 required)

1. Front of CNIC
2. Back of CNIC
3. Selfie holding your CNIC

After you choose a photo, a preview appears in the box. Click the box again to change it.

  • Photos must be clear and show the entire CNIC, with no part cut off.
  • Your selfie must clearly show both your face and your CNIC.
  • JPG, PNG or WEBP only, and each image must be under 5 MB.
  • Your photos are kept private; only the PakGig review team can view them.
  • One CNIC can be used on only one account.
