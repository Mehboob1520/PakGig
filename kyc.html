Identity Verification (KYC)

PakGig par har buyer aur seller ke liye KYC zaroori hai. Is se dono taraf ki hifazat hoti hai.

Tasveerein upload karein (3 cheezein)

1. CNIC ka agla hissa
2. CNIC ka pichla hissa
3. Selfie (CNIC haath mein pakad kar)

Tasveer chunne ke baad box mein uska preview nazar aayega. Badalne ke liye dobara box par click karein.

  • Tasveerein saaf hon, poora CNIC nazar aaye, koi hissa kata hua na ho.
  • Selfie mein aap ka chehra aur CNIC dono saaf nazar aane chahiye.
  • Sirf JPG, PNG ya WEBP, har tasveer 5 MB se choti.
  • Aap ki tasveerein private rakhi jati hain; sirf PakGig ki review team dekh sakti hai.
  • Aik CNIC sirf aik account par istemal ho sakta hai.
