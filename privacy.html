Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

PakGig ("we", "us") operates an online marketplace that connects buyers with freelance sellers. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, password (stored securely by our authentication provider), and profile details you choose to add.
  • Identity verification (KYC): identity documents and related details you submit so we can verify you.
  • Transaction information: orders, messages between buyers and sellers, wallet balances, withdrawal requests, and payment status. We do not store full card or bank credentials; payments are processed by our payment partner, Safepay.
  • Technical information: browser type, device information, and basic usage data used to keep the service secure and working.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and deliver orders.
  • To process payments, hold funds in escrow, release funds, and handle withdrawals.
  • To verify identity, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations.
  • To resolve disputes and provide customer support.
  • To send service-related notices such as order updates and security alerts.

3. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with: (a) the other party to an order, to the extent needed to complete it; (b) service providers that help us operate PakGig, including our payment processor and hosting and database providers; and (c) authorities when required by law.

4. Data security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including encrypted connections and access controls, to protect your data. No online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

5. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to meet legal, accounting, and dispute-resolution requirements.

6. Your choices

You can update your profile information at any time. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us through our Contact page. Some records may need to be retained to meet legal obligations.

7. Cookies and local storage

We use cookies and browser storage only as needed to keep you signed in and remember basic preferences.

8. Children

PakGig is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from minors.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it was last revised.

10. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Reach us via the Contact page.
