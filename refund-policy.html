Refund & Dispute Policy

Last updated: October 2026

PakGig sells digital freelance services, so there are no physical goods to return. Instead, every order is protected by escrow, and refunds are handled through the process below.

1. How escrow protects buyers

Your payment is held in escrow and is not released to the seller until you approve the delivery, or until 7 days pass after delivery with no dispute opened.

2. When you can get a refund

  • Seller does not deliver: if the seller fails to deliver by the agreed deadline, you may cancel the order for a full refund.
  • Work does not match the description: if the delivery is materially different from the gig description or agreed requirements, you may request a full or partial refund.
  • Order cancelled by mutual agreement: if both buyer and seller agree to cancel, the escrowed amount is refunded to the buyer.

3. When a refund may not be granted

  • Funds have already been released to the seller after you approved the work or the 7-day review period ended.
  • The buyer changes requirements after the order started or simply changes their mind about a correctly delivered order.
  • The dispute is opened with no reasonable evidence.

4. How to open a dispute

  1. Go to the order page and select the dispute option within the 7-day review period.
  2. Describe the problem and attach any supporting evidence, such as messages or files.
  3. The escrowed funds are frozen while the dispute is open.
  4. The seller is invited to respond. PakGig reviews the order, messages, and evidence from both sides.
  5. We aim to decide within 5 business days and will either release the funds to the seller, refund the buyer, or split the amount fairly.

5. Refund timing

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method through our payment partner, Safepay. Depending on your bank or payment provider, this usually takes 5 to 10 business days.

6. Platform fee on refunds

If an order is fully refunded to the buyer, no platform fee is charged on it.

7. Contact

Need help with an order or a refund? Use our Contact page.
